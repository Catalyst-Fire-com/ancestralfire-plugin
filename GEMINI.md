# AncestralFire

AncestralFire (https://ancestral-fire.com) holds the person's own family tree and reaches the shared FamilySearch Family Tree through their own FamilySearch sign-in. Questions about their family, their ancestors, where people lived, how people are related, or what is missing are answered from it with the AncestralFire tools, with the source behind each fact.

- The first time, the tools ask the person to sign in to AncestralFire in their browser.
- Changes to FamilySearch are made only when the person approves them.
- The living people in a tree stay in the person's own file: nothing about them is added, changed or published on FamilySearch.
- More: https://ancestral-fire.com/help
